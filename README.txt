MONO Stationery Store

Open index.html in your browser. Each of the 23 products has its own HTML detail page in the products folder. Product images are loaded from the ImgBB URLs supplied by the store owner, so an internet connection is required for those images. Orders submit to Formspree endpoint https://formspree.io/f/xdekryqd. This is a front-end demo; UPI is a payment-method selection only and is not a live payment gateway.

Opening animation: The homepage starts with a short MONO. logo reveal, animated underline, and hero entrance.
